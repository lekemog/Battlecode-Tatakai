## Ranked actions (two; no new bundled strategy)

### 1. Align the deployed danger model's feature contract before interpreting it as probability

**High-confidence static finding; unknown performance impact.**

At this commit:
- `tools/replay/danger.py` builds enemy strength/count/HP from all replay enemies within Chebyshev 8, expressly ignoring walls/bushes. Its invasion-rate feature accumulates **all** enemy presence on the opponent's half across the whole replay each round.
- `bots/v16o/src/bot.cpp` danger_at() L885–899 uses the currently observed enemy vector; L896–897 substitutes **6.76** for invasion rate and side×invasion rate.
- Training standardization mu/sd is computed from all rows before the match holdout split. Labels cover death in the following 30 rounds under historical policies.
- bank() L762 applies p×bag ≥6; L776 uses p >.25 for big-bag danger. A fixed-invasion, visibility-restricted score is not automatically calibrated by a replay-feature holdout AUC.
- The official [C++ API](https://2026a.battlecode.co.uk/game/api), vision namespace, describes fresh visible champion queries and visible occupancy. Global champion identities do not grant hidden live state. Local official SDK `sdk/cpp/cambc.h` also distinguishes global handles from visible runtime properties.

This is **not** a claim the current bot cheats: runtime uses legal observations. The issue is privileged/approximate training features and substituted runtime inputs. Bias direction is unknown: hidden threats, correlated gold/health and the fixed feature can push scores in either direction. AUC does not validate the .25 or expected-loss thresholds. Match holdout is better than row splitting, but repeated opponent/version/map series can still correlate.

**Smallest experiment:** freeze current controller/draft/build and feature weights as A. Offline, recreate B features using exactly the live visibility/age convention, same death-counter reset, constants and numeric scaling; fit preprocessing only on training games. If exact legal visibility cannot be reconstructed, train using only features whose runtime definition can be reproduced, rather than claiming the radius proxy is exact. First run deterministic parity fixtures on hidden/visible enemies, no enemies, a new death and unreachable distance. Retrain/calibrate B on held-out entire opponent series; then change only the banking danger-model component for local both-side comparison. No separate item, draft or retreat changes.

**Acceptance:** zero feature discrepancies in the declared parity fixtures; report held-out Brier/log-loss plus observed death frequency near the actual decision thresholds, with game-cluster uncertainty and missingness. Offline better calibration is a screening result only. Promotion requires repeatable improvement in actual wins/official tiebreaks in Claude's paired external pipeline, no fuel exhaustion and no material farming-race regression. Margins are secondary. If time is short, request the parity check and keep the current selected bot while evidence develops; do not blindly revert the whole banking package.

**Fullhouse transfer:** observable opponent evidence, conservative priors and confidence with sparse data matter more than a sophisticated label. p(death under historical policy) is a risk proxy, not the causal difference between bank-now and farm-now; do not call p×bag a complete action EV. Shrink uncertain local rates toward a prior, and never treat absent sightings as proof of a passive opponent. Aggression, retreat and hoarding require different observations/actions. This is an experiment design, not a demonstrated gain.

### 2. Correct the death audit's attribution and version cohorts before choosing another tactical fix

**High-confidence code limitations; aggregate effect unmeasured.**

`tools/audit/audit.py` blob `4815bfb`:
- L71 treats any positive channelTimer as channel evidence; L80 assigns “tribute_channel” whenever near an altar **and carrying any gems**, even without a tribute channel. Thus “5% died during tribute” is stronger than the classifier establishes.
- L78–85 labels no nearby enemies as camp death; nearby camp plus remaining cases as farm_caught. Neither proves the cause, prior decision, damage source or whether the champion was retreating.
- L74–75 retains any log with rr ≤ t with no age bound, and no sort/max guarantee. Bot log() is sampled every 25 rounds. An old chase label can classify a later unrelated death; nearest log is not the actual last decision.
- L51–56 starts at round 20/21. Counts exclude earlier deaths; “every death” needs that qualification.
- `tools/audit/fetch.py` creates only a createdAt-based “v16m era” boolean, not actual bot version. Its loss-first download order makes the current decoded subset potentially nonrepresentative.
- The report's contest 42→16 comparison changes cohort/opponents/versions; it is encouraging descriptive evidence, not by itself proof “the fix works.”
- “≤2 deaths” means a low-detected-death loss, not proved pure farming: tribute, routing, purchase lag, objective denial and uncounted early deaths remain explanations.

**Smallest experiment:** no bot mutation. Reclassify a blinded sample of five deaths each from alleged tribute/farm/outnumbered classes, plus all first-20-round deaths in those matches. Separate observational flags (near altar/camp, visible enemy count) from verified action/channel/damage source; retain unknowns. Use timestamped max-valid log with explicit age/slot-identity validation. Publish sanitized per-match rows and actual submission IDs, excluding raw private logs.

**Acceptance:** all sampled “verified tribute” labels require tribute-specific state/event; unavailable/old logs remain unknown. Show confusion matrix, early-death exclusions, attempted/decoded/missing counts and match-level cohorts. Recompute contest outcomes/deaths/tribute/gold within comparable map/opponent/side/version blocks. If control data are unavailable, revise the causal wording instead of inventing a control. Select the next tactical ablation from the corrected evidence; do not rerun already rejected blanket caution packages.

## Interpretation and limits

I agree farming throughput deserves priority, but do not accept “remaining gains are farming and targeted caution” as exhaustive. Low deaths do not isolate throughput; 30% “idle” from separate move/attack/cast rates needs a union of productive events and channel/heal/respawn context. actrate.py prints marginal rates, not that union; overlapping move+attack rounds cannot simply be added.

MOBA macro suggests distinguishing safe gold acquisition from objective conversion; micro suggests local danger and coordination should not become permanent team-wide retreat. Sophia-style bounded search/caching and move ordering are useful engineering inspirations, but no new current-game gain is measured here. Existing target-cache/HP/assignment concerns in #6 remain existing work, not new hourly discoveries.
