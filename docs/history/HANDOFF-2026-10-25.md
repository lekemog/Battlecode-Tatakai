## Siege ideas not yet in our bot
All of these come from the MathCosine siege README (the "What siege does" section is worth reading in full) and are ordered roughly by expected value:
1. **Stand-and-fight PvP:**
   - count allies out to 8 tiles;
   - more enemy heads than ours within 6 tiles means don't engage, and leave;
   - one ally within 3 tiles holds a fight that isn't clearly lost (ratio 1.6×);
   - retreat only when `hp < 2.5*threat + 10% max`;
   - hit back against an adjacent melee champion instead of fleeing;
   - ranged champions wait up to 6 turns for enemies to step into range;
   - the team shares a focus target through a shared-memory byte;
   - spells fire before walking.
   On the ladder, siege reports this doubled its kills per game.
2. **Careful mode:** after 3 team deaths in 150 rounds, bank at 10 gems, heal at 55%, score enemy-side camps ×0.15 (squad) and ×0.1 (solo), and treat even fights as lost. Siege went from 454 to 469 on its bench with this.
3. **Safe tributes:** don't start a gem channel that the enemies in reach could kill before it finishes, and cancel one in progress unless the bag wins the game. Our bot already has a partial cancel guard in `play_turn` and a bank `unsafe` check.
4. **Economy:**
   - gold weighs 0.15 relative to gems (our pack `choose_group` uses 1.0);
   - camp scores include the rounds spent walking home to heal the damage the fight will cost;
   - sieges of monoliths over up to 4 trips after round 500;
   - teammates' health is counted as the most each has shown;
   - the `tools/pve.py` farming-speed benchmark runs against an idle bot.
5. **Stuck fixes:**
   - corridor yield: the lower slot goes first;
   - hidden camps;
   - capture claims lapse when the reward is gone, the claimer is 14+ tiles away, or 6 steps fail;
   - walled-off targets are marked unreachable and dropped by the squad;
   - champions stop "retreating" when standing on their own spawn.
6. **Altar locks:** buy one for 10 gold and place it on enemy-side altars or on an enemy that is mid-tribute. **Wards:** dropped on captured groups and at altars.

Ideas from Marcus's `docs/` (v1.5–v1.8.1): endgame banking when the enemy reaches 900 gems, `SQUAD=4`, a bank cap of 45, and carriers retreating at 35%.
