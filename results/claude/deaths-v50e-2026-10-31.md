# #46 champion deaths: why v50c (live v221) dies, and the v50d/e/f candidates

Date: 31 October 2026. Base: `bots/v50c` = 2d93a14 (live submission 221). Local engine 2.0.0, cambc 4.0.0, 70-map pool
from `experiments/map-robustness-20261029/current-maps.zip` (the 40 old maps are byte-identical to `bench/maps`).
Nothing submitted or pushed.

## Reproducing the deaths

Against the 7 bench bots v50c dies 0.6-0.9 times a game, so the bench cannot show the top-ten pattern (11.4 deaths
a game, 45 of 50 lost). v50c against a copy of itself can: on the 8 maps where the top ten killed us 20+ times, the
losing side died 22-26 times in the worst games (contest 26, hooks 24, gold_rush 22).

The v50c death log (10 turns before each death, `tools/scan/deathctx50.py` and the bot's own `death r..` lines):

- most losing-side deaths are 1v1s or 1v2s, often with no ally within 5, at full or high health, against a champion
  with 1.5-2.5x our max health (the snowball: the losing team earned 2,516 gold against 11,454 on contest);
- the forecast reads `s1-0` (we lose one, they lose none) for many turns while the champion keeps farming or
  travelling (`fa/fi`, `fa/tr`) with the enemy 2-9 tiles away;
- `cfg::calm_farm = 1` keeps farming until an enemy can strike next turn. From there an equal-speed chaser is not
  escaped.

## Candidates (each one change on v50c)

| Version | Change | vs v50c, 70 maps x 2 sides | Deaths/game (v50x vs v50c) |
|---|---|---|---|
| v50d | retreat when forecast says we lose and they do not, an enemy within 7 is approaching | 71/140 | 5.53 vs 5.53 |
| v50e | v50d with the raw forecast (the learned edge in `theirs` adds +0.6..0.9 and hid "they lose none"); approach measured every threat turn | 75/140 | 5.26 vs 5.86 |
| v50f | v50e, plus retreat from such an enemy within 5 even when it is not approaching | 55/140 (rejected) | 5.06 vs 5.37 |

v50d almost never fired (logged: the learned edge kept `theirs` at 0.6-1.3 when the raw forecast was 0).

v50e on fresh boards (flipped/rotated copies of the 26 ROTATIONAL180 maps, `mapxform45.mjs`, x1-x3, both sides):
**84/174** wins, deaths 5.62 vs 6.18, gems 720 vs 703. All head-to-head games together: **159/314 (50.6%)**, deaths
about 10% lower in every set.

Regression vs x_daniel8, h_apex, h_mohnish on all 70 maps, both sides (420 games each): v50e 417/420, v50c 416/420;
deaths, kills and gems the same.

## Verdict

v50e is safe (no regression) and reliably removes about 10% of deaths against a strong opponent, but against v50c it
does not win more games. Not worth a ladder slot on this evidence alone. Next: the pinned panel against real top-ten
bots, where deaths are 11.4 a game, would show whether fewer deaths convert there; or pair v50e with a change that
recovers the farming time the early retreats cost.
