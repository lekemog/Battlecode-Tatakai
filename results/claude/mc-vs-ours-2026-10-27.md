## 0. Correcting three premises before the comparison

1. **SOS, "doomed: take a kill first", standoff bans and several fight rewrites exist in MC's code but are OFF in `config.h`**: `sos_radius = 0` (config.h:423), `doomed_kill = false` (398), `standoff_ban_after = 0` (417), `standoff_recall_turns = 0` (433), `tactics_s = 0` (334), `retreat_mode = 0` (299), `phantom_mode = 0` (317), `share_sightings = false` (308), `carrier_capture = false` (453), `hunt_mode = 0` (401), `capture_hold = false` (396), `route_chain = 0` (249), `squad_size = 1` (223). They were tested and rejected or neutral (`DECISIONS.md` D323-D331, tracks/medium.md). So MC's strength does not come from those; do not port them as "MC does X".
2. **The dead-time controls that are ON** are: `fight_watchdog 25` (config.h:270, used brain.cpp:3396-3404), the travel-progress stall detector (15 rounds, brain.cpp:3427-3434), `capture_patience 100` (448), `capture_stall_turns 3` (446), `staging_patience 5` (276), `respawn_wait_liveness` (110, `blocked_respawn` brain.cpp:2203), `planning_turn` (60), `calm_farm 1` (440), `no_loss_stance` (394).
3. **MC's best evidence is mostly local self-play** (their own gauntlet, their own hunters, `hx1`, ES-trained against their family; the local gauntlet is "saturated, 99% vs weak opponents", D360). Online they win 9% vs ranks 1-10, 30% vs 11-30, 43% vs 31-60, 66% vs 61-150, 96% beyond (v44, README). Online A/B arms of their releases were within noise (v50 13, v51 30/250, v52 25/250, v53 32/250 vs top 10; hazard 0.3 +0.2 +- 1.7 pp over 700 matched games, D362). Ladder position (#36, 1989) is real; which of their ~60 mechanisms causes it is not established by any online ablation.

## 2. Port specs (things MC does better), ordered by my expected value

Constants in **bold** are MC's, to be re-tuned on our bench, not copied blindly. All implementations must be written fresh in our style (single `bot.cpp`, `solo_target` / `squad_target` / `play_turn`). "Transfers" = relies on a mechanism our squad core also has; "chassis" = relies on MC's solo-farmer design and needs adaptation.

### A. Multi-trip solo siege with heal cost and camp-HP memory (transfers; highest value)
- **Failure it addresses**: 45% fewer camps, 4 vs 19 elk camps/game, gold 70% of theirs, early gold gap (r50 98 vs 211, r100 266 vs 529 in the 25 Oct top-10 sample, 0.5x; MC's own gap to the top is r100 258/395 = 0.65x, r400 0.57x). Solo champions today only take groups one trip can clear (846) and squads only attempt multi-trip groups after round 500 (792, 973).
- **What to implement** (all in bot.cpp):
  1. Per-camp HP memory: `Group::probe_hp` (vector aligned with `probes`) filled in `observe()` (288-290) from visible monsters; set to 0 on death; reset to base when a group's state flips from reward/respawning back to alive. Sleeping camps keep exact HP [doc, MC handbook note]. `rem_hp()` (890) and `solo_target()` (842) then use remembered HP for unseen members.
  2. `struct TripPlan{int trips; double rounds, dmg, progress;}` from `forecast()` (897): `pool_first = max(0, hp - heal_at*max_hp)`, `pool_next = (1-heal_at)*max_hp` (heal_at is .4/.55 at 1172); `trips = ceil(dmg*margin/pool)` with margin **1.6** (tune 1.0-1.6); feasible if `trips <= 3` (**4** in MC) and the first trip removes `>= 0.35` of remaining HP (`progress = min(1, dps*kEff*pool/(dmg/rounds) / rem_hp)`).
  3. Replace the gate at 846 by `feasible`; replace the cost at 859 by `max(travel,wait) + clear + (trips-1)*ov + frac*ov + capture + .3*altar_d + 2`, `ov = recall_time + 1 + home_d(group)` (walk from spawn), `frac = min(1, dmg/max_hp)` (MC `hp_rounds`, brain.cpp:2300-2308).
  4. Gold weight: parametrise `gold_w` in `solo_target` (805) to {0.15/.08/.04 (current), 0.35, 0.67}. MC uses 1/1.5. Caution: v25w (0.35 early) was neutral on sparring (83 vs 85); retest only together with 2 and 3, because those make elk/wolf groups feasible.
  5. Keep: v25u camp-out and no-recall-in-reach rules (1122-1125, 1173-1184); after a heal recall, `objective` persists (814-820) so the siege resumes without a new decision. Ban the group for 60 rounds after two aborted trips with progress < 10% each.
  6. Keep squad siege (`squad_target`, monolith unlock) unchanged; lower `kSiegeFrom` (792) only in a separate ablation.
- **Risks**: camp deaths were 53 of 113 deaths in 6 siege games before v25u (31 of 39 classified to elks). Multi-trip solo raises exposure to exactly that. Mitigations above. A second risk is more time at low HP near elks with an enemy arriving (MC lives with 13 deaths/game vs top 10).
- **Test plan**: sparring 240 (siege/warlord/marcus) paired with v35a; weak 15-map subset; old pool 501 / new pool 376 baselines. Metrics: camps cleared and elk camps per game, camp deaths, champion deaths (v35a 13.8 on sparring), gold@r100/r400, banks. Gate: >= +15 sparring wins over 480 paired games (two seeds) AND camp deaths <= v35a + 1/game AND no map class (elk_arena, concentrated_wealth, boss_battle, shortcut) loses > 2 of 6. Log `trips` taken and aborted.

### B. Recall package: transport, purse-free shop recall, 50% heal, value-based capture recall (transfers)
- **Failure**: gold 70% (items bought late), 15 deaths/game (heal later than MC), carriers die far from spawn.
- **Implement**:
  1. Travel-as-transport in `play_turn` before the target move (new, near 1285): if `walk(target) > from_spawn(target) + recall_time + 1 + 4`, `enemies.empty()`, `!unsafe_recall`, `!spawn`, then `me.recall()` (**margin 4**, MC `travel_recall_margin`, brain.cpp:3435-3471). Needs a home->group walking distance; `Group::home_d` (103) is already the static walk from our spawn.
  2. Shop recall: drop `me.gem_count()==0` (1196) and replace thresholds by MC's purse rule: gold >= **300** (**150** in the opening, `early_shop_gold` is 0 in MC) and detour `from_spawn + recall + 1 - walk <= 20` (config.h:157-158; 300/20 = 179 vs 163 base). Do the same inside v38a's capture-wait recall (remove its `gem_count()==0`, use detour 0.3 x walk(spawn->group) as the price, MC `capture_recall_walk` 0.3; their later candidate uses 1.0 except near recent enemies, `capture_recall_walk_danger` 0.3, pace -36 rounds, tracks/medium.md).
  3. Heal threshold: sweep `heal_at` .40 / .45 / .50 (non-careful) at 1172; MC found .50 best on their set (config.h:78-81).
  4. Tribute recall as transport (brain.cpp:2096-2113): in `bank()` (before 638) recall when `ad > from_spawn_to_altar + recall_time + 1 + 4` and nothing in view; the bag is kept.
- **Risks**: recall cancelled by damage (handled by `unsafe_recall`); more spawn visits -> more walk-outs. MC's `recall_tail` (recall after a retreat step) lost 46; do not do that.
- **Test**: same benches; metrics: gold spent by r200 (MC 62% -> 75% of earned, top teams ~80%), first shop round (MC 195 -> 163), shop visits by r400 (2.2 -> 2.8 per champion), deaths, time to 1000 gems vs the starter bot.

### C. Dead-time watchdogs and time-use accounting (transfers; cheap)
- **Failure**: 910 squad turns on 02gift-wrapping, farm/stage loops, stuck states.
- **Implement** in `play_turn`: (1) travel progress: remember the best walking distance to the current target; no improvement for **15** rounds while not engaged -> `ban(target, 40)`, clear `objective`/`squad_choose` (MC brain.cpp:3426-3434). (2) engagement watchdog: in `combat(target,...)` path (1285), track the last round an attack/cast landed on this group; **25** rounds engaged without one -> `ban(target, 60)`, and if it was the squad target set `squad_choose = 0` (MC 3396-3404). (3) squad stall: if the squad target is unchanged for N rounds and `alive >= 3` and no champion is within 3 of any of its camps, ban it (`stage` currently allows 12 rounds then re-engages, 1276). (4) counters in the existing `log()` lines every 500 rounds: rounds per activity (dead, recall channel, travel, fight, stage, capture, bank, idle) per champion, so every later variant is judged on time use, not only wins (MC `time_use_` 540-565).
- **Risk**: false bans on big monoliths (long legitimate fights). Count a hit only as a landed attack/cast on that group, and skip the watchdog when `g.monolith`.
- **Test**: the 02gift-wrapping, 02the-pack, 01shortcut and boss_battle maps for stuck turns; then full benches for no regression (expected neutral elsewhere).

### D. Group news in the free shared byte (transfers; constrained by 8 bytes)
- **Failure**: walks to groups that are dead or respawning (14% of MC champion-rounds; ours unmeasured: add a counter first).
- **Implement**: byte 7 (free, see table) = `group_id(6 bits) | counter(2 bits)`. A champion that sees a group in the respawning state (observe, 281-287) and finds that an ally's slot claim (bytes 1-5) or the squad target (byte 0) is that group, writes byte 7 and increments the 2-bit counter; receivers detect a change of the byte, then set `groups[g].ready_round = max(ready_round, round + respawn - 4)` unless they currently see the group (`seen_round == round`). Groups with id > 62 are ignored (check the largest group count on the 70 maps). MC posts the exact timer in 3 bytes; ours is approximate (+-5 rounds), enough to stop the walk.
- **Risk**: a stale post overwritten before it is read; one record only (MC has the same limit). Interaction with learned weights does not exist for us (MC saw +131 on zero weights but only +34 on trained ones).
- **Test**: counter "rounds travelling to a target found respawning/empty on arrival" must fall; wins are expected to move little on sparring, more on the weak subset.

### E. Fight information: enemy memory, calm farm, finish-kill, reactive ally help (partly chassis; moderate risk)
- **Evidence in MC**: `finish_kill 2 + kill_chase 6` +201/-123 (pool+train, 1,920 keys), +178/-107 (heldout+fresh 1,680), kill margin +3 [meas, tracks/medium.md; code in `bots/main/combo2/src/combat.cpp:981`, not yet in v55]; `ally_help 12` +198/-152, deaths 6.4 -> 5.5 [meas]; combined EPF with capture recall full-walk and item-model gem value: +534/-248 over 2,270 unseen paired keys, 63.9% head to head vs v55 [meas, own family + proxy; online untested]. `calm_farm 1` and `no_loss_stance` were adopted to stop standoffs (D168, D171).
- **Implement** (small, one at a time): (1) `EnemyMem[8]` (pos, hp, strength, focus, round) set in `observe()`; in `fight()` (1048-1050) enemies seen <= 5 rounds ago within 6 + age tiles join `theirs` (config.h:305-311). (2) `no_loss_stance`/`calm_farm`: when the power ratio says we are not outmatched (not `lost`, not `danger`), do not retreat from an enemy that cannot strike next turn; keep farming (MC brain.cpp:1752-1790). (3) finish-kill: before the verdict in `fight()` (1051), if a visible enemy off its spawn has `hp + shield <= burst` of basic + ready damage/cc/aoe spell + reset (plan: reuse the spell loop in `combat()` 456-501), kill it from where we stand (melee may step one tile), in every branch including retreat; chase only channeling carriers or 2-hit kills up to 6 tiles. (4) ally help: if an ally within 12 has an enemy within 3 of it and our hp >= 50% and we are not capturing, `move_to(disk(ally,2))` (MC brain.cpp:3825-3860).
- **Chassis caveat**: our `fight()` already makes the team commit through a shared focus; MC's verdict is per champion. Ally help and finish-kill are independent of that. A full port of `simulate_skirmish` is not recommended first (MC's own rewrite lost 82 wins).
- **Test**: sparring 240 + a hunter-like sparring bot; metrics kills per game (ours 0.6 vs top-10 15.5), deaths, kill margin; abort if deaths rise > 1/game.

### F. Patch 4.0 shop and capture handling (transfers; see section 3)
Replace the Treasure opener; designate a Capture/Prospector holder; use `capture_state()` instead of `constants::capture_time` (5) in all cost formulas (859, 915, 980, 1018, 855) and in v38a's hold counter. Details in section 3.

### G. Draft re-check on 4.0 stats (data; cheap; see section 3)
One-line order changes in `draft.cpp` (default 5-pick, size 3/4 default maps) tested on sparring and the new pool.

### H. Wards on bushes and trails (transfers; low value)
Drop the free ward every time `ward_cooldown == 0`, prefer a bush within placement radius (MC brain.cpp:2802-2885, mode 4). Our version only places at altars/captures (1146-1150). Expected: fewer unseen-attacker deaths and better group beliefs (wards "keep respawn in sight"). MC: +13/174 vs hunters; neutral vs own versions.

## 5. Honest limits

- **I read code and notes; I ran nothing.** All MC effect sizes are their numbers from their engine copy (3.0.5, engine 1.0.3), their opponents (their own earlier versions, `hx1`, `hunter7`, `compound-field-growth`, `gank`, `ball`) and their map sets (pool/train/heldout/legacy/fresh). Gates are paired keys, 1,500-2,300 per claim, p-values are theirs. None was reproduced.
- **Online evidence for any single mechanism is absent or null.** Hazard model: +198/-134 locally, +0.2 +- 1.7 pp online (D362). The learned layer: +344/-175 broadly vs v52 locally, online arms within noise (25-32 of 250). `capture_handoff` (+209/-143 locally) was only checked online with v54x vs v53; v55's own online effect is unmeasured in the files. Their ladder rise (#62 at 1871 before v50 to #36 at 1989) coincides with v50-v55 but changes were bundled.
- **The "be grouped" lever contradicts their own rejected cohesion.** Cohesion bonus in the target score was -119..-233 wins (crowding, slower); grouped farming, pairs and opening balls were rejected (tracks/medium.md). The version that helped is **reactive**: `ally_help 12` (walk to an ally already in contact), `help_engaged_allies`, `stand_with_engaged_allies`. Our squad design is proactive grouping; MC's evidence argues for keeping it for monoliths/herds only and using reactive help elsewhere. D347's "the lever is to be grouped" refers to contact states in the death census, not to farming together.
- **Death counts are not clearly better for MC.** They die ~13/game vs the top 10 (our 18.2 sample), 4 vs their local opponents. Our 15 deaths/game vs mid-rank teams' 3 has no MC counterpart number for the same opponents; I cannot say MC is at 3 there.
- **Gold ratios use different top samples** (MC: top-15 by r100/r400 in D326; ours: top-10 on 25 Oct). The 0.65x / 0.57x vs our 0.5x / 0.33x comparison is indicative.
- **Which chassis-specific parts matter is unknown.** MC's score formulas run inside a solo-farmer design where `committed` allies divide the reward; their ES-trained weights are co-adapted (they measured that reshaping the score breaks the fit, tracks/medium.md). The multi-trip siege and heal-cost terms are plain formulas and should transfer, but in our code they replace gates tuned over many versions; v39a could lose on maps with hand-fit behaviour (the ~20 fingerprints, 24-33 in `init()`).
- **Unmeasured on our side**: fuel exhaustion rate of v35a (I found none logged), the actual share of walks to dead groups, v38a-f results, the 02gift-wrapping stuck cause (I only have your 910-turn figure), and whether v36a/v37a improve on v35a. The sparring set (240 games, 90 wins, SE about 5-6 wins) cannot confirm effects below ~+15 wins; the 1,500-key standard from MC (D360) is out of reach on one machine, so combine paired seeds and read secondary metrics (camps, deaths, gold, stuck turns).
- **4.0 mechanics still unknown** (also open for MC): whether the Capture reduction applies when the holder joins mid-capture (two texts conflict), Collectors' damage scope, actual Skirmish effect, Prospector rounding in a multi-holder capture, `contested` vs `enemy_capturing` transitions. One probe game per item before building rules on them.
- **MC's snapshot predates 4.0 play.** Nothing in `bots/main` buys a 4.0 item or reads `capture_state()`. Their claim to strength on 4.0 is untested; their advantage on the new 70-map pool is unknown.
