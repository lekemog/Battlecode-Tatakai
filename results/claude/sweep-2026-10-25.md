# Bot sweep, 25 Oct: what the bot does poorly and what can get us into the top 50

Live bot: `bots/v25m`. Evidence comes from `data/scrim_cache*.json` (20 scrims vs the top 4), `data/fish_cache_1890540d.json` (300 games of the #1 team), the SDK headers (`cambc 3.0.5`, engine 1.0.3), and new local runs.

## 1. The gap, in numbers (top-4 scrims, v24z, 20 games, 0 wins)

| | Us | Top 4 |
|---|---|---|
| Gold at r100 | 212 | 396 (1.9x) |
| Gold at r400 | 987 | 2363 (2.4x) |
| Deaths per game | 14.5 | 1.6 |
| Deaths to camps per game | 3.0 | ~0 |
| Camps killed per game | 126 | 186 |
| Elk camps killed per game | 4 | 19 |
| Banks per game | 17 | 37 |

The economic gap opens before the fights do. At r100 they already have 1.9x our gold, and kills can't explain that. Deaths then compound it: each death costs **60 rounds out of the game + 100 gold to the killer + the bag** (constants read from the engine: `respawn_time=60`, `kill_gold=100`).

## 2. Bugs and blind spots found in the live code

1. **Golems and clusters have the wrong stats** (`camp_stats_for`, `bot.cpp:21`). Golems (5000 HP, 120 strength, range 4) fell through to the default case: 500 HP, 0 strength. The bot rated them as harmless, quick camps. Gem and gold clusters were costed at 500 HP instead of 120. Golems appear on 6 ranked maps: great_wall, lost_world, quadrants, reverse_spiral, road_of_riches and split. Those are exactly the maps that later needed per-map hacks (forced solo, special drafts, "caution"). **Fixed in v25n.**
2. **We never draft or play Heart of Ruin.** The top 4 picked it in 20/20 scrims, and the #1 team in 96% of 300 games. The top-4 comp is HoR, Vesper, Ryth, Marcella, Vendetta. HoR was only ever tried inside a different comp (v24q), with no HoR-specific play:
   - Its dash (1.5x focus area hit + 1-turn stun, `cd=8`) was never used offensively. `combat()` never casts dashes, and `move_to()` spends every dash on walking.
   - `focus_build` ignored the dash, so HoR got a strength build. The #1 team builds it Treasure-focus → Home-health → Durability-health, then Power-focus (8 Power emblems by r800).
   - **Fixed in v25o.** The draft is changed in v25p.
3. **No Home emblem anywhere.** Home cuts recall from 5 turns to 3, and the #1 team's carries buy it early. Our traces show champions dying mid-recall.
4. **Fight evaluation is inconsistent** (`handle_enemies`, `bank`). On every map except eight:
   - our own power is `dps()*hp`;
   - allies and enemies are `strength*1.5*hp`, so focus counts for nothing.
   Our Vesper/Tavi casters are undervalued (we evade too often), and enemy Stellar/Cassi/HoR are undervalued (we take bad fights). v19 tried to recalibrate this, but changed the threshold at the same time and lost.
5. **"Stand and fight" down to 12% HP** (`bot.cpp:1251`). Once an enemy is in range and no dash is ready, the champion fights whether or not the fight is winnable. We die at a mean of 13% HP. The real fix is upstream: don't get caught in range, which means deciding on sight.
6. **Enemies are forgotten the moment they leave vision.** `threat()` and `danger_at()` only see enemies visible this turn. There is no memory of last-seen positions. Shared memory is only 8 bytes and all of it is used, so this has to be per-champion memory.
7. **Dead code.** `update_profile()` computes TURTLE/DISRUPT/SAFE modes behind `if(false)`.
8. **Elk avoidance.** The solo safety check (`str*clear*.6 > hp*k`) rules elks out. Top teams take 19 a game.

## 3. Process problems (why ~200 versions moved the bench but not the ladder)

- **The bench is saturated.** 482/560 = 86% wins against 7 frozen bots, while ranked is ~50% and the top 4 are 0/20. Changes are accepted on ±2-game moves, and one standard deviation over 80 games is about ±4.5 wins.
- **About 20 map fingerprints** (exact width/height/altars/groups/camps) hold per-map farming modes and drafts, tuned on that same bench. Any pool change sends a new map to untested defaults. Some of these rules were patching the golem bug.
- **We copy the outputs of strong teams (drafts, item lists) without the play that makes them work.** v18o/v22w (Ryth) and v24q (HoR) were "rejected" because our controller can't play those champions, not because the picks are bad.

## 4. What can get us into the top 50 (ordered by expected value)

1. **Ship the bug fixes:** v25n (golem/cluster stats), plus v25o (HoR dash and focus build, inert until HoR is drafted).
2. **Make HoR actually work, then draft the top-4 comp.** Test on scrims vs teams ranked ~50-150, not on the weak bench.
   - Next steps for HoR: open fights with the dash-stun, and lower HoR's retreat threshold (its damage grows +50% as its health drops).
3. **Cut deaths from ~14 to under 5 a game.**
   - Buy Home emblems (3-turn recall).
   - Keep a 3-5-round memory of last-seen enemies, so evasion starts on sight instead of on contact.
   - Value focus consistently for allies and enemies (focus weight ~0.5, **thresholds unchanged**).
   - Measure only deaths per game and gold at r400 in scrims vs ranks 50-150.
4. **Close the early-economy gap.** Allow elk camps when the champion has ≥X HP and Jungle-health, and bank smaller bags more often (37 banks vs our 17).
5. **Retire map fingerprints one by one.** Re-test each against the fixed generic code; keep only the ones that still win.
6. **Change the test loop.** Add strong sparring bots to `bench/bots` (the Marcus and siege bots used on 25 Oct are not in the repo). Judge on head-to-head plus deaths and gold@r400, not on bench wins.
