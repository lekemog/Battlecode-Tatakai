## Reading

- The cap helps in the mirror (more banked, fewer gems lost on death) with a sensible dose response: 40 best,
  60 smaller gain, 30 too small (more trips). The gain is modest (53.5% over 314 games).
- The mirror cannot show the top-10 failure (the opponent guarding its altar while our carriers hoard): our own bot
  does not guard. The top-10 panel is the test that matters for v50m1.
- v50m3 again cuts deaths (-8%) and gems lost (-24%) without winning the mirror. The team found the mirror penalises
  caution (v226's own change was 41% in the mirror and won the strong panels), so v50m3 also deserves a panel run.
